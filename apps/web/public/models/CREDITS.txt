3D trophy models for prizes (plan 14a)
=======================================

The first five files were generated by this project from plain geometry (lathe
profiles, tori, spheres, boxes and an extruded star) with the script
apps/web/scripts/build-trophies.mjs:

    node apps/web/scripts/build-trophies.mjs

No third-party meshes, textures or other assets are included in those five.

grail.glb — a custom, one-off «family grail» trophy: project-generated gold
geometry (a lathed goblet, tori, a nameplate) plus three baked texture images —
a ring of the owner's own family photos and two engraved captions. Built with
three.js + GLTFExporter from the owner's photos (used with permission); the
photos are the owner's and are NOT CC0. The geometry is CC0 1.0.
