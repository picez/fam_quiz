3D trophy models for prizes (plan 14a)
=======================================

The six files below were generated by this project from plain geometry (lathe
profiles, tori, spheres, boxes and an extruded star) with the script
apps/web/scripts/build-trophies.mjs:

    node apps/web/scripts/build-trophies.mjs

No third-party meshes, textures or other assets are included in those six.
